# Screenshot guide for submission

Capture the actual running prototype at desktop width. Do not use fabricated screenshots.

## Student before/after
1. Sign in as a student with no active booking; capture the eligibility/progress card and booking call to action (before).
2. Book a future slot; capture the confirmation and updated dashboard (after).
3. Capture the student attempt-history view after an interviewer submits a result.

## Interviewer
4. Sign in as the interviewer assigned to the test booking; capture the daily schedule and availability list.
5. Open the interview evaluation modal; capture the four-criterion 1–5 scorecard and outcome options.

## Admin
6. Capture operations metrics, eligible/unbooked counts and interview records.

## Evidence hygiene
- Keep passwords and evaluator credentials out of screenshots.
- Use only fictional demo records.
- Label the screenshots as prototype/demo screens.
- The login screen should show only email, password and Sign In; credentials are shared separately with the evaluator.
